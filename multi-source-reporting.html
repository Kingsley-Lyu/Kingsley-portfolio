Multi-Source Client Reporting

A small reporting prototype exploring how data from multiple platforms could be brought into one consistent client view.

Synthetic data — a conceptual prototype, not an analysis of any specific company's real accounts.

This prototype aims to explore a pattern that shows up in multi-client agency work: performance data often sits across several platforms, each with its own structure and metric definitions. Having worked on similar data integration and reporting challenges in financial services, I built this to examine how the same approach could apply here.

Illustrative flagging logic

Simple, explainable rules — not a statistical model. The point is transparency: anyone reading the output can see exactly why an account is flagged.

Healthy
  • No sustained deterioration in ROAS or CPA
Watch
  • ROAS declining for 2+ consecutive periods, or
  • CPA increasing materially
At risk
  • ROAS declining for 3+ consecutive periods, and
  • CPA increasing materially

These thresholds are illustrative and would be validated against historical performance in a real implementation.

Potential value

A consolidated reporting layer could reduce repetitive monthly data preparation, provide a consistent view across accounts, and help surface accounts that may warrant further investigation before a client review. No time savings are claimed here since none have been measured.

Before using this in production, I'd validate
